# Ebola outbreak risk-communication guide

> **Development fixture — not approved for public release.** Messages require authorization and current verified epidemiological information.

## Communication principles

- Be accurate, timely, empathetic and explicit about uncertainty.
- Use plain language and accessible formats in relevant local languages.
- Protect patient, contact and health-worker privacy.
- Correct harmful rumours without repeating or amplifying them unnecessarily.
- Direct communities to verified services and response contacts.

## Message approval

Confirm the data-as-of time, source, spokesperson, audience, translation review and release channel. Clinical advice must be reviewed by an authorized clinician; epidemiological figures must be cleared by incident management.

## Community feedback

Provide a route for questions and concerns, log recurring information needs, share them with response leadership, and update messages when verified guidance changes.

## Correction protocol

When published information is wrong or outdated, issue a clearly labelled correction through the same channels and retain an auditable record of the superseded message.
