# Ebola response training guide

> **Development fixture — not a competency certification.** Training must use current approved materials and qualified facilitators.

## Role-based preparation

Assign learning objectives for triage, IPC, clinical care, laboratory work, transport, surveillance, communication and coordination according to each participant's role.

## Practice

Use supervised simulation for alert handling, isolation, PPE procedures, specimen handover, referral communication and exposure escalation.

## Competency check

Assess observable performance with the current approved checklist. Record gaps and repeat supervised practice before assigning independent duties.

## Updates

Brief teams whenever the case definition, clinical protocol, IPC procedure, reporting form or coordination contact changes.
