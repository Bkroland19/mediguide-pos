# Ebola case-management standard operating procedure

> **Development fixture — not approved for clinical use.** Replace this document with the current clinician-approved Ministry of Health SOP before any production publication.

## Purpose

Provide a consistent demonstration workflow for receiving, isolating, assessing and referring a person who meets the active suspected-case definition.

## Immediate actions

1. Keep a safe distance and prevent unnecessary contact.
2. Move the patient to the designated isolation area without crossing clean zones.
3. Apply the current risk-assessed PPE procedure.
4. Notify the district surveillance focal person and designated treatment unit.
5. Record only the minimum information required for safe handover.

## Clinical assessment

- Check airway, breathing, circulation, mental status and hydration using appropriate IPC controls.
- Identify urgent supportive-care needs and exposure risks.
- Avoid procedures that are not clinically necessary or cannot be performed safely.
- Follow the activated response pathway for testing and transfer.

## Referral and handover

Confirm that the receiving team is ready, use approved transport, document the handover, and initiate environmental decontamination and exposure follow-up.
