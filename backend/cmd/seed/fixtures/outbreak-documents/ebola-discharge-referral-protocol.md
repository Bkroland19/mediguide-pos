# Ebola discharge and referral protocol

> **Development fixture — not approved for clinical use.** Only the activated national protocol and authorized response team can determine discharge, referral or follow-up requirements.

## Referral readiness

Confirm acceptance by the receiving team, safe transport, required IPC measures, minimum necessary documentation and a named handover contact.

## Discharge review

An authorized clinician must confirm that the current clinical, laboratory and public-health criteria are met and documented before discharge.

## Handover

Explain follow-up arrangements using accessible language, protect confidentiality and provide verified contact routes for new symptoms or concerns.

## Follow-up

Record the responsible team, schedule, monitoring period and escalation triggers from the active protocol. Never invent a follow-up interval when the approved source is unavailable.
