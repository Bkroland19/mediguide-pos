# Suspected Ebola case health-worker checklist

> **Development fixture — not approved for clinical use.** Use the active Ministry of Health case definition and response contacts.

## Immediate checklist

- [ ] Stop unnecessary contact and keep the patient in a safe, separated area.
- [ ] Notify the facility IPC focal person and district surveillance focal person.
- [ ] Apply risk-assessed PPE and hand-hygiene procedures.
- [ ] Record symptoms, onset, travel, contact and exposure history.
- [ ] Assess urgent clinical needs without unsafe procedures.
- [ ] Confirm the designated referral and transport arrangement.
- [ ] Identify staff, patients or visitors who may have been exposed.
- [ ] Secure the area for approved cleaning and waste management.
- [ ] Complete confidential handover and incident documentation.

## Safety pause

If the correct isolation space, PPE, trained staff or referral pathway is unavailable, stop and escalate to the response coordinator rather than improvising an unsafe process.
