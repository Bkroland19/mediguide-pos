# Ebola screening and clinical-management pathway

> **Development fixture — not approved for clinical use.** Treatment decisions require the activated clinician-approved protocol and appropriately trained staff.

## Screening and triage

Apply the current case definition, identify immediate threats to life, limit exposure and activate the designated response pathway.

## Decision pathway

1. If the case definition is not met, follow routine care while documenting the assessment.
2. If the definition may be met, isolate safely and notify the response team.
3. If urgent supportive care is required, provide it using the approved IPC controls and escalation pathway.
4. Arrange authorized testing, referral and handover without unsafe transport.

## Clinical management

Use the current national treatment protocol for monitoring, fluids, medicines, investigations and complication management. Verify all doses, contraindications and patient-specific decisions against the approved source.

## Review

Document clinical changes and reassess the care plan at every handover or material change in condition.
