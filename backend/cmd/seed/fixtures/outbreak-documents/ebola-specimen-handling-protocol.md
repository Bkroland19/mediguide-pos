# Ebola laboratory specimen-handling protocol

> **Development fixture — not approved for clinical use.** Specimen collection and transport must follow the activated national laboratory protocol.

## Before collection

1. Obtain authorization through the outbreak coordination pathway.
2. Confirm the receiving reference laboratory and transport plan.
3. Assemble approved collection, labelling, triple-packaging and spill-response materials.
4. Verify patient identifiers using the minimum necessary data.

## Collection and packaging

- Use trained staff and the current risk-assessed PPE procedure.
- Label the primary container before leaving the controlled area.
- Decontaminate the primary container as prescribed.
- Complete leak-proof secondary and rigid tertiary packaging.
- Keep request documentation outside contaminated packaging.

## Transport and handover

Use the authorized courier route, maintain required conditions, document chain of custody, and obtain laboratory receipt confirmation. Escalate leaks, damage, delay or temperature excursions immediately.
