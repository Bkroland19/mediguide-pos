# Ebola suspected-case definition

> **Development fixture — not approved for clinical use.** Apply the currently activated Ministry of Health case definition; do not use this demonstration text to classify a patient.

## Recognition

Use the active national definition to assess symptoms, onset, epidemiological links, travel, contact with a suspected or confirmed case, healthcare exposure and relevant animal or funeral exposure.

## Immediate action

If the active definition may be met, prevent unnecessary contact, use the designated isolation pathway, apply risk-assessed IPC precautions and notify the authorized surveillance focal person.

## Documentation

Record the definition version and data-as-of time used for the assessment. Preserve uncertainty and never alter findings merely to force a classification.

## Reassessment

Reassess when new exposure information, symptoms, laboratory results or an updated national definition becomes available.
