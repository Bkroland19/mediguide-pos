# Ebola contact-tracing field guide

> **Development fixture — not approved for clinical use.** Apply the current national contact definition, follow-up interval and escalation protocol.

## Identify and register

- Interview the case or an appropriate proxy using respectful, non-stigmatizing language.
- Identify contacts across household, healthcare, transport, workplace and community settings.
- Record the minimum necessary identity, exposure, location and follow-up information.
- Assign a stable contact identifier and responsible tracing team.

## Daily follow-up

Confirm wellbeing using the approved method, record completion promptly, protect confidentiality, and escalate symptoms or a missed-contact risk according to the active protocol.

## Alert escalation

Do not direct a symptomatic contact to routine public transport or an unprepared facility. Notify the designated alert desk and follow instructions for safe assessment.

## Closure

Close follow-up only after the nationally defined monitoring period and supervisor review. Retain records according to the approved privacy and retention policy.
