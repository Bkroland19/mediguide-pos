# Ebola infection prevention and control SOP

> **Development fixture — not approved for clinical use.** Replace with the current nationally approved IPC SOP before production use.

## Core controls

- Establish clearly marked clean, transition and patient-care zones.
- Restrict access to trained, assigned staff.
- Perform hand hygiene at every required moment.
- Select PPE from the current risk assessment and approved procedure.
- Use a trained observer for donning and doffing.

## Environmental cleaning

Prepare disinfectant at the approved concentration, clean from lower-risk to higher-risk areas, manage sharps immediately, and place infectious waste in the designated stream.

## Exposure or PPE breach

Stop safely, wash the affected area as directed, report immediately, document the incident confidentially, and enter the approved occupational-exposure pathway.

## End-of-shift check

Confirm that waste, reusable equipment, environmental cleaning, supply replacement and exposure reports have been handed over.
