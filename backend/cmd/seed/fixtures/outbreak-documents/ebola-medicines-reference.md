# Ebola medicines and supportive-care reference

> **Development fixture — not approved for prescribing.** This fixture intentionally contains no medicine doses. Use only the current clinician-approved treatment protocol and formulary.

## Before prescribing

Confirm patient factors, indication, allergies, contraindications, interactions, organ function, pregnancy status where relevant and the current approved protocol.

## Verification

Independently verify medicine, formulation, route, dose, frequency and duration. Escalate any discrepancy or unavailable reference to the responsible clinician or pharmacist.

## Monitoring

Record the intended outcome, safety monitoring, adverse-event pathway and the time of the next clinical review.

## Supply continuity

Use authorized supply channels and document substitutions. Do not substitute a medicine or concentration without clinical and pharmacy approval.
