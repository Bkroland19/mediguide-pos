# Ebola response forms and documentation guide

> **Development fixture — not an operational form.** Use the current Ministry of Health forms and approved secure systems.

## Required forms

- Suspected-case notification and investigation form
- Laboratory request and chain-of-custody documentation
- Referral and clinical handover form
- Contact registration and follow-up record
- Exposure or IPC incident report

## Minimum necessary data

Collect only information required by the approved workflow. Protect identifiers, restrict access and follow the applicable retention policy.

## Quality check

Confirm form version, completeness, date and time, responsible officer and safe delivery to the authorized recipient.
