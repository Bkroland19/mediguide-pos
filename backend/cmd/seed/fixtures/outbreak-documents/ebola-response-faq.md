# Ebola response frequently asked questions

> **Development fixture — not approved for public release.** Verify answers against current Ministry of Health and WHO sources before use.

## What should a health worker do when Ebola is suspected?

Prevent unnecessary contact, follow the designated isolation and IPC pathway, and notify the authorized surveillance focal person using current response contacts.

## Where are current case definitions and protocols found?

Use the approved documents published in this response hub. Check their issuing authority, effective date, review date and publication status.

## Can this application replace clinical judgement?

No. MediGuide provides access to governed information and decision support. Apply professional judgement and escalate uncertainty through approved clinical and public-health channels.

## How should incorrect or outdated information be handled?

Do not continue using it. Report the issue, use the latest approved source and preserve an auditable correction or supersession record.
